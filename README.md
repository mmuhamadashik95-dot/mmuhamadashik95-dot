## About

I'm a **B.Pharm student (2024–2028)** at **Rathinam College of Pharmacy, Coimbatore**, and the **Founder of Prescripsy** — an AI-powered prescription-safety venture registered under the Government of India's **MSME (Udyam)** scheme.

Prescripsy screens a patient's **complete medicine list** for drug–drug interactions, flagging risky combinations for pharmacist review **before dispensing** — closing the gap between counters that nobody else sees.
